📄 Resume Tips

  • Keep your resume simple and professional.
  • Add education and relevant skills.
  • Mention internships and academic projects.
  • Include relevant certifications.
  • Check spelling and grammar.

🎤 Interview Questions

Tell me about yourself.

Prepare a short introduction about your education, skills, projects and goals.

What are your strengths?

Choose genuine strengths and give an example.

Why should we hire you?

Explain your skills and willingness to learn.

🌟 Career Tips

  • Learn useful skills before graduation.
  • Build small projects.
  • Apply for internships.
  • Practice interviews.
  • Keep your resume updated.
  • Improve communication skills.
